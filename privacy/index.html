Privacy policy

Trust Me — Fais-moi confiance (Android and iOS) · Last updated: November 13, 2026 · Version française

Publisher: Mouldi Bouchami · Contact: bmouldi@gmail.com

In short

  • No account, no name, no email: the game never asks you for anything personal.
  • Your progress, your settings and, if you give it, your year of birth stay on your device. The publisher never receives them.
  • The game is free thanks to ads from Google AdMob. A one-time "No ads" purchase removes them.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows full-screen ads between some levels, and an optional video to skip a level. To show them, choose them, limit how often they repeat, measure their performance and fight fraud, Google may collect:

  • the device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID, or IDFA on iPhone if you allow it);
  • the IP address, and therefore an approximate location;
  • technical information (model, system, language) and your interactions with the ads;
  • diagnostic data from the ad SDK.

Google processes this data under its own policy: policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses data from partner apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices

  • Europe (EEA, UK, Switzerland): on first launch, a form asks for your consent to personalised ads. You can change your mind at any time: home screen → PRIVACY → MY AD CHOICES. Without consent, Google only shows non-personalised or limited ads.
  • iPhone: iOS asks whether the game may "track" you. If you refuse, the IDFA is not shared. You can change this in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Under 16: if you say so, ads are not personalised and the game does not show Apple's tracking request.

"No ads" purchase

The purchase is handled entirely by Google Play or the App Store: the game never receives your name, your email or your payment details. It only learns from the store that the purchase exists, and records it on your device. After the purchase, the game shows no more ads and no longer loads the ad SDK at startup.

Children

The game is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data about them.

Legal basis (GDPR)

Personalised ads: your consent. Non-personalised ads and fraud prevention: the legitimate interest of funding a free game.

Your rights

Advertising data is held by Google: you can exercise your rights of access, rectification and erasure with Google (policies.google.com/privacy). For any question about the game: bmouldi@gmail.com. You may also contact your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL: cnil.fr).

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page is updated.
